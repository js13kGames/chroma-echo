# 🌈 CHROMA ECHO

**Every mistake leaves a rainbow.**

CHROMA ECHO is a magical puzzle adventure where **your past mistakes become your greatest allies**.

You play as a tiny unicorn exploring a mysterious world. As you move, your journey is recorded as a glowing rainbow trail.

But here's the twist:

## 🌈 Rewind. Remember. Collaborate.

Press **R** to rewind, and your previous journey doesn't disappear.

It returns as a **CHROMA ECHO** — replaying your past movements alongside you.

Use your Echoes to activate magical switches, open blocked paths, collect stars, and solve puzzles that would be impossible alone.

**Your failures are not erased. They become part of the solution.**

---

## 🎮 Controls

### Desktop

* **WASD / Arrow Keys** — Move
* **R** — Rewind and create a CHROMA ECHO
* **Enter** — Start / Continue
* **M** — Toggle sound

### Mobile

* **Touch / Drag** toward the direction you want to move
* Tap to start and continue

---

## ✨ Technical Highlights

Built entirely with:

* HTML5 Canvas
* Vanilla JavaScript
* Procedural graphics
* Procedural Web Audio
* No external game engine
* Desktop and mobile support
* Offline gameplay

And the entire game fits into a **tiny 13KB competition package**.

## 🦄 The idea behind CHROMA ECHO

Most games treat failure as something to erase.

CHROMA ECHO does the opposite.

**Every attempt becomes a memory.**

**Every memory becomes an Echo.**

**And every Echo can help you move forward.**

🌈 **Play. Fail. Remember.**
